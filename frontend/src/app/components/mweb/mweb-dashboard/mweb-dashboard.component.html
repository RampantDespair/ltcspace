MWEB Explorer

MWEB (MimbleWimble Extension Block) is Litecoin's opt-in privacy layer, activated in May 2022. Litoshis pegged into MWEB are spent in a confidential side-chain where amounts and addresses are hidden, and transactions are aggregated into a single block-level digest. This page tracks MWEB supply, peg-ins, peg-outs, kernels, and recent activity as data emerges on-chain.
